FHU SNEAK OUT

Curfew's in effect. Get across campus without getting spotted by security.

Desktop: WASD / Arrow keys to move. Mobile: touch and drag anywhere to steer with the on-screen joystick.

Avoid the yellow vision cones. Duck behind trees, pillars, tables and walls to break line of sight. Reach the green exit to move to the next area.

Purple watchtowers sweep their cone in a full circle and never move — time your dash for the gap in the sweep instead of beelining for the exit.

Badger Gym has a pool — moving near the water can make noise and draw a guard even outside their cone.

Every area has an objective (gold diamond) you must grab before the exit unlocks: Commons — paint the lion statue. Gardner — retrieve your bible. Badger Gym — retrieve clothing from the middle of the pool. Brown Kopel — retrieve your scholarship. Lloyd Auditorium — put on your shorts. Gano/Lions Pride — check your mail.

Score is total time survived. The further you get, the higher it climbs.
